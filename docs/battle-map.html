🗺️ Battle Map Engine

Grid, Movement & Range

The battle map is a rectangular grid where each square represents 5 feet, matching the D&D 5e ruleset. Every creature occupies one square, and all movement, range, and line-of-sight calculations are resolved on this grid each turn.

Movement

Distance is calculated using Chebyshev distance (diagonal movement costs the same as cardinal), which is the standard for D&D 5e on a square grid. Each step along a planned path is charged individually so difficult terrain and opportunity attacks are applied at the right moment.

Opportunity Attacks: when a creature leaves a square threatened by an enemy, that enemy may use its reaction to make an immediate attack before the move is committed.

Range & Attack Validation

Melee Range

Default 5 ft. (one square). Extended to 10 ft. for reach weapons.

Ranged — Normal

Attack rolls within normal range have no penalty.

Ranged — Long

Attacks between normal and long range are made with disadvantage.

Point-Blank

Firing a ranged weapon while an enemy is adjacent imposes disadvantage.

Map Definition

Maps are defined inline in the scenario JSON. Walls and difficult terrain are specified as rectangles [col, row, width, height], and creature starting positions are keyed by name.

Example: "walls": [[5, 0, 1, 8]] places a wall 1 tile wide and 8 tiles tall starting at column 5, row 0 — splitting the map in two.
